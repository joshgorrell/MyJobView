# Purchase Order and Deposit Payment Workflows

## Overview

The proposal approval system now supports flexible PO and deposit workflows that match real-world business scenarios.

## 1. Purchase Order Workflow

### The Problem
Customers often approve a proposal with a PO payment method, but don't have the PO number yet because:
- PO is still being generated by their accounting
- They need to submit paperwork internally first
- The PO will arrive later via email or mail

### The Solution
**PO number is now optional** - You can approve with PO method without the number.

### How It Works

#### Option A: PO Number Available Now
1. Customer/sales rep approves proposal
2. Selects "Purchase Order" payment method
3. **Enters PO number** (e.g., "PO-2025-0145")
4. Optionally uploads PO document PDF
5. ✅ System creates sales order immediately in "Planning" status
6. Ready for scheduling

#### Option B: PO Number Not Available Yet
1. Customer/sales rep approves proposal
2. Selects "Purchase Order" payment method
3. **Leaves PO number blank**
4. Optionally uploads PO document PDF
5. ⏳ Proposal goes to "Approved - PO Pending" status
6. Sales rep gets notification: "PO Number Needed"
7. **Later**, when PO number arrives:
   - Customer OR sales rep enters the PO number
   - System automatically creates sales order in "Planning" status
   - Sales rep gets notification: "PO Number Received"
   - Ready for scheduling

### Where to Add PO Number Later

**For Customers (Portal):**
- Go to Proposals tab in customer portal
- Click on the proposal
- Enter PO number in the designated field
- Optionally upload PO document

**For Sales Reps (Internal):**
- Go to Proposals page
- Find the proposal (filter by "PO Pending" if needed)
- Click to view/edit
- Enter PO number in the designated field
- Optionally upload PO document
- System automatically creates sales order

## 2. Deposit Payment Workflow

### The Problem
When a deposit is required, customers need an easy way to pay, and sales reps need to record payments.

### The Solution
**Automatic invoice creation and payment processing** with multiple payment options.

### How It Works

#### Scenario A: Deposit Required (Not Paid Yet)
1. Customer/sales rep approves proposal
2. Selects "Payment" method
3. System automatically:
   - Creates deposit invoice (e.g., $500 deposit on $5,000 job)
   - Sets invoice status to "Sent"
   - Creates sales order in "Pending Deposit" status
   - Sends deposit invoice to customer (if email enabled)

4. **Customer pays deposit:**
   - **Option 1: Online via Portal**
     - Customer logs into portal
     - Views invoices
     - Clicks "Pay Now" on deposit invoice
     - Pays via QuickBooks integration (credit card/ACH)
     - System automatically:
       - Marks invoice as "Paid"
       - Updates sales order to "Planning" status
       - Notifies sales rep: "Deposit Payment Received"
       - Ready for scheduling

   - **Option 2: Sales Rep Records Payment**
     - Customer pays via check, cash, or offline credit card
     - Sales rep goes to Invoices page
     - Finds deposit invoice
     - Clicks "Record Payment"
     - Enters payment details (amount, method, date, reference)
     - System automatically:
       - Marks invoice as "Paid"
       - Updates sales order to "Planning" status
       - Ready for scheduling

#### Scenario B: Deposit Already Paid (In Person)
1. Customer sits with sales rep and pays deposit immediately
2. Sales rep approves proposal
3. Checks "Deposit received" box
4. System automatically:
   - Creates deposit invoice marked as "Paid"
   - Creates payment record
   - Creates sales order in "Planning" status (ready immediately)
   - No additional steps needed

## 3. No Deposit Required Workflow

Some customers don't require deposits (established accounts, small jobs, etc.)

1. Sales rep approves proposal
2. Selects "No Deposit" action
3. System automatically:
   - Creates sales order in "Planning" status
   - Ready for scheduling immediately
   - No invoice created (billing happens after job completion)

## Comparison: Before vs After

### Before (Old System)
- **PO Approvals:** Required PO number immediately, caused delays
- **Deposit Payments:** Manual coordination between sales and customer
- **Payment Recording:** Sales rep had to remember to update multiple places
- **Sales Orders:** Often created late or forgotten

### After (New System)
- **PO Approvals:** Can proceed without PO number, add it later
- **Deposit Payments:** Customers can pay online directly
- **Payment Recording:** Automatic status updates across the board
- **Sales Orders:** Created automatically based on settings

## Key Features

### 1. Automatic Sales Order Creation
When a proposal is approved (regardless of method):
- Sales order is created automatically
- Status set appropriately based on payment situation
- All linked correctly (proposal → sales order → invoice)

### 2. Smart Status Management
- **PO Pending** - Approved but waiting for PO number
- **Pending Deposit** - Approved but waiting for deposit payment
- **Planning** - Ready for scheduling (deposit paid or not required)

### 3. Automatic Notifications
Sales reps get notified when:
- Customer approves proposal in portal
- PO number is needed
- PO number is received
- Deposit payment is received
- Any status changes occur

### 4. Flexible Payment Recording
Sales reps can record payments for:
- Check
- Cash
- Credit card (offline)
- Wire transfer
- QuickBooks payment link (for online payments)

## Database Changes

### New Fields Added

**proposals table:**
- `po_pending` (boolean) - Tracks when PO number is still needed
- `po_document_url` (text) - Storage URL for uploaded PO document

### New Triggers

1. **complete_po_pending_approval** - Automatically creates sales order when PO number is added
2. **handle_deposit_payment_completion** - Automatically updates sales order when deposit is paid

### New Notification Types

- `po_number_needed` - Notify sales rep when PO approval happens without number
- `deposit_invoice_paid` - Notify sales rep when deposit is paid

## UI Updates

### Portal (Customer View)
- **ProposalApprovalModal:** PO number field now optional with clear messaging
- **Invoices:** "Pay Now" button for online payments
- **Proposals:** Can add PO number to approved proposals

### Internal (Sales Rep View)
- **ApprovalActionModal:** PO number field optional, can approve without it
- **Proposals List:** Filter/indicator for "PO Pending" proposals
- **Invoices:** "Record Payment" for offline payments
- **Notifications:** New notification types for PO and deposit events

## Best Practices

### For Sales Reps

1. **When Customer Doesn't Have PO Yet:**
   - Go ahead and approve with PO method
   - Don't wait for the PO number
   - You'll get notified when it's needed
   - Add it as soon as customer provides it

2. **When Recording Deposit Payments:**
   - Use "Record Payment" on the invoice
   - Include reference number (check #, last 4 of card, etc.)
   - System handles the rest automatically

3. **When Customer Pays In Person:**
   - Check "Deposit received" during approval
   - System creates everything as paid
   - No follow-up needed

### For Customers

1. **If You Don't Have PO Number Yet:**
   - Approve the proposal anyway
   - Select "Purchase Order" method
   - Leave PO number blank
   - Add it later when you receive it

2. **For Paying Deposits:**
   - Log into customer portal
   - Go to Invoices tab
   - Click "Pay Now"
   - Use credit card or ACH
   - Instant confirmation

## Automated Prompting and Notifications

The system actively prompts customers and sales reps to complete required actions.

### For Customers

**1. PO Number Needed Email**
When a customer approves with PO method but doesn't provide a number:
- Automatic email sent to customer
- Subject: "Purchase Order Number Needed - Proposal [NUMBER]"
- Clear call-to-action button to customer portal
- Instructions for adding PO number online or via sales rep

**2. Deposit Payment Request Email**
When a deposit invoice is created:
- Automatic email sent to customer
- Subject: "Deposit Payment Required - Proposal [NUMBER]"
- Shows deposit amount prominently
- "Pay Deposit Online" button to customer portal
- Lists other payment methods (check, cash, wire)
- Shows invoice details and balance after deposit

### For Sales Reps

**1. PO Number Needed Notification**
When customer approves without PO number:
- Notification: "PO Number Needed"
- Message: Customer approved but didn't provide PO number
- Action: Follow up with customer to obtain PO

**2. PO Number Received Notification**
When PO number is added later:
- Notification: "PO Number Received"
- Message: PO number received, sales order created
- Sales order automatically ready for scheduling

**3. Deposit Payment Received Notification**
When deposit invoice is paid:
- Notification: "Deposit Payment Received"
- Message: Deposit paid, sales order moved to Planning
- Ready for scheduling

### Notification Timing

**Automatic (Immediate):**
- PO request email → Sent when approval happens without PO number
- Deposit request email → Sent when deposit invoice created
- Sales rep notifications → Sent immediately when status changes

### Email Templates

Two new professional email templates added:
1. **po_request** - Prompts customer to provide PO number
2. **deposit_payment_request** - Prompts customer to pay deposit

Both templates:
- Mobile-responsive design
- Clear call-to-action buttons
- Professional gradient headers

### Sales Rep Dashboard

Sales reps can track pending actions in proposals list:
- Filter by "PO Pending" status
- Filter by "Pending Deposit" status
- See days pending for each proposal
- Sort by urgency
