## Requested behavior

- Initial contracts offer **12, 24, 36, 48, and 60 months**, defaulting to 36. The selected value is actually saved. Renewal remains independent of the initial term.
- Staff and customer onboarding use white controls with black text, including native light checkboxes in a dark workspace.
- Printing a blank form is read-only and requires no existing customer or contract. Print Paper Form selects a template, services, and initial term; the customer fills the resulting form by hand. Enter Completed Paper Form starts staff web entry only after the form is returned. Printing does not create, submit, approve, or activate a contract. There is no new paper-upload requirement. Entered agreements use the same form validation, verified payment prerequisites, transactional saves, and approval states.
- Submitted contracts are locked by default. An authorized Admin or manager with Contract Management access clicks a small edit icon, changes a field, records a reason, and chooses Save or Cancel. Saved fields relock. Corrections return the contract to pending approval.
- Editable onboarding fields include customer name/email/phone, service address, emergency contacts/codewords/authorization, account classification, monitoring and service account numbers, installation date, monitoring services, price, initial/renewal terms, cancellation notice, billing preference, terms, payment selection, signature/signing date, tax classification, invitation override, and notes. The mailed-billing exception retains its existing Admin-only permission.
- Corrections retain the original submission/signature and an append-only revision history with actor, timestamp, reason, and before/after data. Concurrent stale edits fail. Current printable copies identify staff corrections, and the original submission remains printable. Internal IDs, tenant ownership, original evidence, and approval provenance are not editable form fields.
